ASIM coverage depends on which parsers exist

The schemas are product-neutral, but a source only gets ASIM content once someone writes its parser.

ASIM parser exists
Long tail with no parser
Niche SaaS apps, in-house applications, custom _CL tables, vendor solutions that ship only their own rules

Shape is illustrative, not measured
A parser exists
Every ASIM analytics rule, hunting query and workbook covers the source with no extra work.
No parser exists
ASIM content returns nothing for the source. Someone has to write and maintain a parser: Microsoft, the vendor, the community or you.
The vendor solution ships only its own rules
Those rules query the raw table directly. They do not feed or use ASIM content.
